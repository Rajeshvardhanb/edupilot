# Feature Scope

Proposed changes on `sandhya`. Awaiting approval; no features removed. Checkboxes track completed removals and will be updated after verification.

## Features to Keep

- **Admin Login** — Secure sign-in for the administrator to manage the application.

- **Dashboard** — A quick overview of student numbers, attendance, marks, and results.

- **Student Registration** — Add new students with their personal details, roll number, and class.

- **Student Management** — View and edit student profiles, contact details, class, and status.

- **Search Student** — Quickly find a student by name, roll number, or class filters.

- **Marks Management** — Add assessments and enter or update each student's subject marks.

- **Student Result** — Calculate grades and view individual student results.

- **Attendance Management** — Mark and correct attendance for students by class, date, and period.

- **Attendance Report** — View attendance percentages and identify students with low attendance.

- **Production Safeguards** — Keep password recovery, security checks, data validation, activity logs, backups, error handling, mobile-friendly screens, and relevant tests. Basic class, subject, and exam records stay where required.

## Features to Remove

- [ ] **Other User Logins** — Remove department head, teacher, accountant, office staff, student, and parent accounts and dashboards. Only admin access stays.

- [ ] **Fees and Payments** — Remove fee structures, invoices, collections, receipts, refunds, scholarships, concessions, instalments, fines, online payments, ledgers, day closing, and no-dues statements.

- [ ] **Faculty and Staff Management** — Remove teacher profiles, workload, teaching history, staff attendance, leave, appraisal records, salaries, payroll, and payslips.

- [ ] **Advanced Academic Workflows** — Remove teacher assignments, semester promotion, detention, graduation, and elective selection. Basic class and subject records stay.

- [ ] **Timetable Management** — Remove weekly schedules, timetable editing, clash checks, substitute teachers, and class swaps.

- [ ] **Advanced Examination Services** — Remove exam scheduling, halls, seating, invigilation, hall tickets, supplementary registration, revaluation, and grace marks. Basic assessments, marks, and results stay.

- [ ] **Learning and Course Activities** — Remove study materials, assignments, submissions, assignment grading, syllabus coverage, course feedback, and learning outcome reports.

- [ ] **Student Leave and On-Duty Requests** — Remove student leave, medical leave, and on-duty application and approval workflows. Regular attendance marking stays.

- [ ] **Placements and Alumni** — Remove recruiter records, placement drives, applications, interviews, resume profiles, offer letters, placement statistics, and graduate tracking.

- [ ] **Library, Hostel, and Transport** — Remove book catalogues, loans and fines, hostel rooms and allotments, bus routes, stops, and passes.

- [ ] **Inventory and Campus Entry** — Remove equipment tracking, asset issue/return, visitor entry/exit records, and student gate passes.

- [ ] **Office and Student Services** — Remove admission enquiries and follow-ups, certificates, ID cards, batch document generation, profile correction requests, and parent contact logs. Student registration and admin editing stay.

- [ ] **Announcements and Reminders** — Remove notices, circulars, notification inbox, WhatsApp messages, bulk reminders, and emails for removed features. Password recovery and operational alerts stay.

- [ ] **Additional Reports** — Remove the custom report builder, accreditation reports, and scheduled email reports. Attendance reports and student results stay.

- [ ] **Optional App Features** — Remove offline attendance syncing, app installation and offline caching, language switching, guided tours, and dashboard customization.
